### Game concept discussed

- Simulate financial life for ~50 NPCs with persistent preloaded data and reset options.
- Events happen automatically through months/years.
- Events should vary by probability and timing; some are one-off events, not recurring annually.
- Add seasons/map changes over time.

### Gameplay ideas

- Player makes decisions at each time point, rather than all upfront.
- Bankruptcy/homelessness triggers feedback on what went wrong.
- Replay/rewind could let players revisit decisions and try alternatives.
