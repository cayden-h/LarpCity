# E2E pass (2026-09-13)

## Goal

Larp City works end to end with no discrepancies.

- Flow: Home screen -> Learn -> save slots screen -> Sammy walks the player through their goals and the age to meet each -> the city.
- Random life events fire about half as often as before.
- Every UI surface is readable and easy to reach, on desktop and phone widths.
- There are no other states: any state hash (#TX, #dallas, ...) falls back to the home state, California (San Francisco).
- Every button, phone app, and the Money desk are walked through in the browser.
- The onboarding never asks for job, salary, rent, debt, or savings: the game generates them (age 22, randomized salary and debt, 600 credit score, per the revamp meeting); the player only picks the health plan, the starter card, and their goals.
- Testing comes first and every issue is logged here; fixing starts after the testing phase, then each fix is re-verified in the browser.

## Status

Fixed and re-verified in the browser unless noted:

- Flow: a plain visit opens the title screen; Learn (or "Skip to save slots") opens a full-screen save slot picker; an empty slot (or "New life", confirmed twice) starts Sammy's setup; a saved slot resumes. `?slot=` in the URL (a reload, a demo link) boots straight in. "Start a new life" in the Calendar restarts Sammy's setup in the same slot.
- Sammy's setup (`ui/intake.ts`, 9 steps with Back / Next): name and avatar, the generated life (job, salary, card and loan, car loan, savings, 600 score), health plan, starter card, then one step per goal with its age (retire by, debt-free by, own a home by with the down payment, married by or "Not for me"), and a review. No money questions; the voice interview and typed form are gone.
- Name, avatar, plan, card, and goals now reach the life (findings 3); the Goals app shows each target age.
- Events: every rate in `EVENT_RATES` halved (about 13 events in a 40-year life, down from 26).
- States: the hash is set to `#CA` on boot and after any change; saves from other states load in California; the U.S. map, its travel, and the city thumbnails are removed (the Map app shows San Francisco only).
- Readability: unspoken words in Sammy's bubble at 70% (was 35%); visible selected states on every picker; Back / Next buttons instead of underlined links; the Map card's city name wraps.
- Dropped: the Money desk time dock "covering content" (its pages already pad 96px for it, so content scrolls clear).
- Not changed: the Calendar's day chips truncate long labels in small cells (full text is in each day's label); News waits up to 15s per Gemini model before its fallback.

## Findings

Found while testing, before any fixes.

### Flow

1. After Learn, the game goes straight to the intake; there is no save slots screen in the flow (slots only open from the Calendar or `?slots=1`).
2. Goals are the last intake screen, with no Sammy narration, and the house goal has no target age.
3. The goals and name picked in the intake are thrown away: `main.ts` turns the intake into a profile, then `answersFromProfile` rebuilds the life with the name "You" and `DEFAULT_GOALS`.

### States

4. `#TX` in the URL is honored: the intake backdrop is Houston and the city opens in Texas (`player.place` is TX).

### Readability

5. Sammy's bubble draws unspoken words at 35% opacity (`narrator.css`); when the voice doesn't play, whole lines stay light gray on cream and are hard to read (Learn and the arrival line).
6. The insurance, starter card, and expense pickers show no selected state; every option looks the same.
7. The insurance and card screens' "Continue" is only a small underlined link.
8. The typed money form's right-hand inputs overflow past the card's right border at 1440px.
9. The expenses screen is taller than a 723px-high viewport; the Move in button is cut off at the bottom.
